## Article 2 — Vision, Mission and Objectives

### 2.1 Vision

To establish Netrokona University as a recognized center of excellence in competitive programming, computational problem-solving, software development, web development, cybersecurity, and other related areas of computing in Bangladesh.

### 2.2 Mission

To nurture algorithmic and computational problem-solving skills; to foster competence in software development, web development, cybersecurity, and related areas of computing; to prepare and support students for national and international programming and computing contests; and to build an inclusive, collaborative, and supportive community of learners in computer science and related fields.

### 2.3 Objectives

1. Organize regular programming contests, workshops, training sessions, and other learning activities for students of all skill levels.
2. Prepare and support students and teams representing Netrokona University in ICPC, IUPC, hackathons, software development competitions, cybersecurity competitions, and other national and international programming and computing events.
3. Build and maintain a repository of learning resources covering algorithms, data structures, competitive programming, and related problem-solving techniques.
4. Foster peer-to-peer learning and mentorship between senior and junior students.
5. Promote the inclusion, participation, and advancement of women in engineering, programming, and related fields through dedicated initiatives and activities.
6. Organize seminars, industry talks, technical sessions, and interactions with alumni, professionals, and practitioners in computing and related fields.
7. Represent Netrokona University at inter-university, national, and other relevant programming and computing events.
8. Maintain transparent, democratic, and accountable governance in all affairs of the Club.

# Article 6 — Duties and Responsibilities

## 6.1 President

The President shall:

* Serve as the chief executive of the Club and preside over meetings of the Executive Committee and General Body.
* Act as the Club's principal official representative and spokesperson before the University and external bodies.
* Exercise a casting vote in the event of a tie, except where otherwise provided by this Constitution.
* Hold final signing authority on official Club documents, jointly with the General Secretary or Treasurer where required or specified.
* Delegate specific responsibilities or portfolios to other Executive Committee members as necessary for the effective functioning of the Club.
* Ensure that this Constitution, the decisions of the General Body, and the resolutions of the Executive Committee are upheld in all Club activities.

## 6.2 Vice President

The Vice President shall:

* Assist the President in the performance of executive functions.
* Assume and perform the duties of the President in the President's absence or inability to act.
* Oversee specific portfolios or responsibilities delegated by the President or Executive Committee.
* Coordinate among the various Secretaries and Executive Committee members to ensure effective cross-team planning and execution.
* Perform any other duties assigned in accordance with this Constitution.

## 6.3 General Secretary

The General Secretary shall:

* Serve as the administrative head of the Club.
* Maintain official administrative records, correspondence, meeting notices, minutes, and other official documents of the Club.
* Issue official notices and correspondence on behalf of the Executive Committee.
* Serve as custodian of the Club's official documents, including this Constitution and its amendments.
* Coordinate with the Membership Secretary to ensure consistency between administrative records and the official membership records of the Club.
* Perform other administrative duties assigned by the Executive Committee.

## 6.4 Treasurer

The Treasurer shall:

* Maintain accurate and up-to-date financial accounts and records of all Club income, expenditure, assets, and liabilities.
* Collect membership dues, sponsorship funds, donations, and other lawful income of the Club.
* Prepare the annual budget and present financial statements and reports at General Body meetings and whenever required by the Executive Committee.
* Manage Club funds in accordance with the approved budget, financial decisions of the Executive Committee, and the provisions of this Constitution.
* Serve as joint signatory with the President for withdrawals or authorized transactions involving Club funds, where applicable.
* Shall not approve, authorize, or process any payment to themselves. Any such payment shall require the counter-signature or approval specified under Clause 9.2.
* Preserve all relevant financial records and supporting documents for audit, review, or inspection as required.

## 6.5 Organizing Secretary

The Organizing Secretary shall:

* Plan, coordinate, and execute Club events, including contests, sessions, workshops, seminars, and other approved activities.
* Coordinate event logistics, including venue arrangements, scheduling, equipment, materials, and volunteer assignments.
* Work with other Secretaries and relevant Executive Committee members to ensure the effective execution of events.
* Liaise with the Department, University authorities, and other relevant bodies for necessary approvals, facilities, and institutional support.
* Prepare or coordinate post-event reports where required.

## 6.6 Associate Organizing Secretary

The Associate Organizing Secretary shall:

* Assist the Organizing Secretary in planning, coordinating, and executing Club events and activities.
* Assist with event logistics, volunteer coordination, and operational arrangements.
* Assume the duties of the Organizing Secretary when the Organizing Secretary is absent or unable to perform their duties.
* Perform other responsibilities assigned by the Organizing Secretary or Executive Committee.

## 6.7 Programming Secretary

The Programming Secretary shall:

* Design and coordinate competitive-programming training sessions, practice activities, and educational programs for Club members.
* Curate, prepare, or recommend practice problems and other competitive-programming resources.
* Organize internal programming contests, including mock ICPC-style contests and other competitive events.
* Maintain appropriate records of members' participation and competitive-programming development for training and team-selection purposes.
* Coordinate the process for selecting or recommending teams and participants for programming competitions, subject to any applicable rules or decisions of the Executive Committee.
* Work with relevant Executive Committee members to promote competitive-programming and related technical skill development among Club members.

## 6.8 Associate Programming Secretary

The Associate Programming Secretary shall:

* Assist the Programming Secretary in preparing training materials, practice activities, and programming contests.
* Assist in maintaining the Club's problem archive, contest materials, judging systems, and related technical resources.
* Support the operation and administration of training sessions and internal contests.
* Assume responsibilities delegated by the Programming Secretary or Executive Committee.

## 6.9 Membership Secretary

The Membership Secretary shall:

* Lead the recruitment of new members and administer the membership admission process in accordance with Clause 3.3.
* Maintain the official membership database and records of members' status and standing under Clause 1.7.
* Coordinate with the General Secretary to ensure that membership records remain accurate and consistent with the Club's administrative records.
* Organize orientation, onboarding, and initial engagement activities for newly admitted members.
* Inform the Executive Committee of significant changes in membership status where required under this Constitution.

## 6.10 Associate Membership Secretary

The Associate Membership Secretary shall:

* Assist the Membership Secretary in member recruitment and outreach.
* Assist in processing membership applications and maintaining relevant membership records.
* Assist with orientation, onboarding, and engagement activities for newly admitted members.
* Perform other membership-related responsibilities delegated by the Membership Secretary or Executive Committee.

## 6.11 Publicity Secretary

The Publicity Secretary shall:

* Manage the Club's public communications and promotional activities.
* Manage and coordinate the Club's official social media content and other public-facing communication channels.
* Design, prepare, or coordinate promotional materials for Club events and activities.
* Handle routine external media and press inquiries relating to Club communications, in coordination with the President where official representation or policy statements are involved.
* Work with other Executive Committee members to promote Club activities, achievements, and announcements.

## 6.12 Associate Publicity Secretary

The Associate Publicity Secretary shall:

* Assist the Publicity Secretary in managing public communications and promotional activities.
* Assist in creating and distributing social media content and promotional materials.
* Support publicity and communication activities during Club events.
* Perform other communication-related responsibilities delegated by the Publicity Secretary or Executive Committee.

## 6.13 Women's Engagement Secretary

The Women's Engagement Secretary shall:

* Plan and lead initiatives that encourage and support the participation of women in engineering, programming, and related Club activities.
* Organize seminars, mentorship programs, workshops, outreach activities, and other initiatives relevant to women's engagement.
* Identify and communicate barriers or concerns affecting women's participation within the Club.
* Represent matters relating to women's engagement and participation in Executive Committee discussions and decision-making.
* Coordinate with other Executive Committee members to promote an inclusive, supportive, and respectful Club environment.

## 6.14 Information Secretary

The Information Secretary shall:

* Maintain and update the Club's website and official internal information channels.
* Manage the technical organization and accessibility of official Club information made available to members.
* Document and archive photographs, videos, event records, and other historical materials relating to Club activities.
* Disseminate official information and updates to members on behalf of the Executive Committee through appropriate information channels.
* Coordinate with the General Secretary regarding official records and with the Publicity Secretary regarding public-facing communications.
* Take reasonable measures to preserve the accuracy, accessibility, and continuity of the Club's digital information resources.

## 6.15 Additional Activities and Allocation of Responsibilities

The Club may introduce, organize, conduct, or participate in additional activities as approved by the Executive Committee. Such activities may include, but are not limited to, activities relating to web development, software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, data science, robotics, other technical fields, competitions, contests, workshops, seminars, training sessions, projects, outreach programs, and similar activities.

* The Executive Committee shall determine the nature, scope, and objectives of any additional activity.
* Responsibility for planning, coordinating, organizing, promoting, administering, or supporting an activity shall be assigned to the Executive Committee member or members whose existing responsibilities most closely relate to that activity.
* Where an activity involves multiple areas of responsibility, the relevant Executive Committee members shall coordinate and jointly carry out their respective duties.
* The Executive Committee may delegate responsibilities to Associate Secretaries, appointed officers, coordinators, committees, teams, or other members as necessary for the effective implementation of an approved activity.
* The Executive Committee may establish temporary committees, teams, projects, or coordination groups for specific activities.
* The introduction, modification, or discontinuation of an activity shall not require an amendment to this Constitution, provided that such action is approved by the Executive Committee and does not conflict with this Constitution.

## 6.16 Appointed Officers and Coordinators

The Executive Committee may appoint additional officers, coordinators, or other persons where necessary for the effective operation of the Club and the implementation of its activities and projects.

Such appointed positions may include, but are not limited to:

* Graphic Designer
* Software Development Coordinator

* Technical Coordinator
* Event Coordinator
* Project Coordinator
* Content Creator
* Media Coordinator
* Photography Coordinator
* Outreach Coordinator
* Any other position considered necessary by the Executive Committee

The Executive Committee shall determine the title, duties, scope of authority, duration of appointment, and reporting structure of each appointed position.

Appointed officers and coordinators shall:

* Perform the specific duties assigned to them by the Executive Committee or by the relevant Executive Committee member responsible for the applicable area of work.
* Report to and remain accountable to the Executive Committee.
* Provide progress reports, activity reports, or other information regarding their assigned responsibilities whenever requested by the Executive Committee.
* Work under the supervision and direction of the relevant Executive Committee member or members.
* Cooperate with Executive Committee members, Associate Secretaries, committees, and other members as necessary to carry out their assigned responsibilities.
* Remain subject to the decisions, policies, and directions of the Executive Committee.

Unless otherwise expressly provided by this Constitution, appointed officers and coordinators:

* Shall not be considered members of the Executive Committee solely by virtue of their appointment.
* **Shall have no voting rights in Executive Committee meetings or decisions.**
* Shall not be counted for the purpose of determining the quorum of the Executive Committee.
* Shall not exercise any constitutional authority reserved exclusively for elected or otherwise constitutionally recognized Executive Committee members.
* May attend Executive Committee meetings only when invited, requested to report, or when their attendance is necessary for matters relating to their assigned responsibilities.
* May participate in discussions relating to their assigned responsibilities when permitted by the presiding officer or Executive Committee, but such participation shall not confer any voting right.

The Executive Committee may create, modify, suspend, replace, or discontinue an appointed position and may appoint or remove an appointed officer or coordinator in accordance with the applicable decisions and procedures of the Club.

The creation, modification, or discontinuation of an appointed position shall not require an amendment to this Constitution, provided that such action does not conflict with any provision of this Constitution.

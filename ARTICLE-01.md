# Article 1 — Name, Nature, Affiliation and Scope

### 1.1 Name

The organization shall be known as the **Netrokona University Programming Club**, abbreviated as **NeUPC** (hereinafter referred to as the “Club”).

### 1.2 Nature

NeUPC shall be a voluntary, non-profit, non-political, and non-discriminatory student organization dedicated to competitive programming, software development, and computer science education. The Club shall promote learning, collaboration, leadership, creativity, problem-solving, and the overall development of its members.

The Club shall operate in accordance with this Constitution, its approved policies, and the applicable rules and regulations of Netrokona University.

### 1.3 Affiliation

NeUPC shall operate as an independent student organization under the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Netrokona University, with its own administration, membership, activities, and decision-making. The Club shall remain subject to the applicable rules, regulations, and policies of the University.

### 1.4 Scope of Activities

The activities and affairs of the Club shall primarily focus on programming, software development, competitive programming, and related areas. The Club may also organize or participate in coding competitions, hackathons, technical workshops, seminars, conferences, and other academic, technical, professional, or related activities, both within and outside the University, in accordance with the objectives of the Club.

### 1.5 Legal and Institutional Compliance

The Club shall comply with all applicable laws, rules, regulations, and policies of Netrokona University. No provision of this Constitution shall be interpreted as permitting the Club or any of its members to act contrary to such requirements.

### 1.6 Official Identity

The official name, logo, and other identifying marks of the Club shall be used only for legitimate Club purposes and in accordance with the rules established by the Club and the relevant institutional authorities.

## Article 10 — Code of Conduct and Disciplinary Action

### 10.1 Expected Conduct

Members shall act with integrity, respect, and professionalism in their interactions with other members, the University, and external parties. Members shall represent the Club and the University responsibly and shall refrain from plagiarism, cheating, dishonesty, and other unethical conduct in contests, academic activities, and Club activities.

### 10.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following conduct is prohibited:

1. harassment, bullying, discrimination, or ragging;
2. misuse, unauthorized use, or misappropriation of Club funds, property, accounts, or other resources;
3. cheating, plagiarism, contest manipulation, or other forms of dishonesty in Club activities or competitions;
4. unauthorized actions taken in the name of the Club;
5. conduct that seriously damages the Club's activities, operations, or legitimate interests; and
6. conduct that violates applicable University rules, policies, or codes of conduct.

### 10.3 Disciplinary Procedure

1. **Complaint:** A complaint concerning a member shall be submitted in writing to the Executive Committee. A complaint concerning the President shall be submitted to the Advisory Panel.

2. **Investigation:** The Executive Committee shall appoint an investigation committee of at least three (3) members who are not involved in the matter. No person who is the subject of the complaint or has a direct conflict of interest shall participate in the investigation or decision.

3. **Right to Respond:** The member concerned shall be informed of the allegations and given a reasonable opportunity to respond before any disciplinary decision is made.

4. **Decision:** After considering the complaint, investigation findings, and response of the member, the appropriate authority shall determine whether disciplinary action is warranted.

5. **Sanctions:** Depending on the seriousness of the matter, sanctions may include a formal warning, suspension of Club privileges, removal from an Executive position, suspension or termination of membership, or referral to the Department of Computer Science & Engineering.

6. **Departmental Referral:** Matters that cannot be appropriately resolved within the Club, or that involve serious misconduct, financial misconduct, or significant risk to members or the Club, may be referred to the Department of Computer Science & Engineering for further action.

7. **University Referral:** Matters that fall within University-level disciplinary procedures, or that cannot be resolved at the Departmental level, may be referred to the appropriate University authority.

### 10.4 Appeal

A member aggrieved by a disciplinary decision may submit a written appeal to the Advisory Panel within seven (7) days of the decision. The Advisory Panel shall review the appeal and may uphold, modify, or overturn the decision.

Where the matter falls within the authority of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering or the University, the member may further appeal or seek review in accordance with the applicable Departmental or University procedures.

### 10.5 Interim Suspension

Where a complaint under Clause 10.3 involves an immediate risk to the safety of members, Club funds, or Club operations, the President may impose an interim suspension of the accused member's Club privileges pending investigation. If the President is the subject of the complaint, the interim suspension may be imposed by the Executive Committee by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote.

An interim suspension shall not constitute a finding of guilt and shall be reviewed within fourteen (14) days. The accused member shall retain the right to be heard during the disciplinary process.

### 10.6 Protection Against Retaliation

No member shall be penalized, excluded, threatened, or otherwise subjected to retaliation for filing a good-faith complaint under Clause 10.3 or for cooperating with an investigation. Retaliation shall itself constitute a violation of this Article and may result in disciplinary action. A complaint alleging retaliation shall be handled in accordance with the disciplinary procedures established under this Article.

### 10.7 Contest Integrity — High-Stakes Contests

Cheating in a High-Stakes Contest shall be treated as a serious violation of this Code of Conduct. For the purposes of this Article, a "High-Stakes Contest" includes:

1. any contest organized by the Club, including internal trials and team-selection contests;
2. an ICPC preliminary or qualifying round; and
3. an ICPC or IUPC main or regional contest,

where a member participates individually or as part of a Club-affiliated team.

Cheating includes, but is not limited to:

1. copying or plagiarizing another person's or team's solution without authorization;
2. giving or receiving unauthorized assistance during a contest;
3. submitting under a false identity, using multiple accounts, or having another person compete on one's behalf;
4. using resources, software, tools, or other assistance prohibited by the applicable contest rules; and
5. manipulating contest results, judging systems, or scoreboard data.

A finding of cheating in a High-Stakes Contest established through the disciplinary procedure under Clause 10.3 shall result in expulsion from the Club and disqualification from holding an Executive Committee position or representing the Club in future competitions. The member shall not be eligible for readmission for a period determined under the disciplinary provisions of this Article.

The sanction shall remain subject to the appeal procedure under Clause 10.4 and shall be independent of, and in addition to, any sanction imposed by the organizers of the relevant contest.

Cheating in other contests shall remain prohibited under Clause 10.2 and shall be addressed through the disciplinary procedures under this Article.

### 10.8 Disciplinary Ladder for Other Contests

Cheating in a contest that does not meet the definition of a High-Stakes Contest under Clause 10.7, including an individual contest on platforms such as Codeforces, AtCoder, or similar platforms that is not organized by the Club, shall be addressed through the following disciplinary scale.

1. **First Offense:** A formal written warning shall be issued and recorded in the member's disciplinary record. The member may also be required to attend an integrity briefing or counseling session designated by the Club. The member may be made ineligible for selection to a Club-affiliated external team for one contest cycle.

2. **Second Offense:** The member may be suspended from Club privileges for up to one academic semester. If the member holds an Executive Committee position, the Executive Committee may also consider removal from that position in accordance with Clause 5.4.

3. **Third Offense:** A third confirmed offense may result in suspension or expulsion from the Club, depending on the circumstances and severity of the conduct.

4. **Serious or Organized Cheating:** Any single offense involving organized or large-scale cheating, including operating or participating in a coordinated cheating scheme, may result in immediate expulsion from the Club where established through the disciplinary procedure under Clause 10.3.

The disciplinary scale applies throughout a member's entire membership in the Club and does not reset at the beginning of a new academic year.

Each disciplinary decision shall remain subject to the appeal procedure under Clause 10.4. Club disciplinary action shall be independent of any separate action taken by the relevant contest platform or organizer.

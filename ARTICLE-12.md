# Article 12 — Miscellaneous Provisions

### 12.1 Interpretation

In case of ambiguity or dispute concerning the interpretation of this Constitution, the Executive Committee may issue an initial interpretation for the purpose of Club administration. Where the matter concerns the powers of the Executive Committee, the validity of an election, a disciplinary matter, or a dispute between constitutional bodies, the matter shall be referred to the Advisory Panel for interpretation.

Any interpretation shall remain subject to applicable University and Departmental rules and policies.

### 12.2 Supersession

This Constitution supersedes all prior written or unwritten rules, conventions, practices, and resolutions of the Club to the extent that they are inconsistent with this Constitution.

Any existing rule or practice that is not inconsistent with this Constitution may continue to apply until amended or replaced in accordance with this Constitution.

### 12.3 Effective Date and Adoption

This Constitution shall be formally adopted by a simple majority vote of the General Body and shall be signed by the current Executive Committee and the Advisory Panel. The Constitution shall take effect on the date of such approval.

### 12.4 Severability

If any provision of this Constitution is found to be invalid, unenforceable, or inconsistent with applicable University or Departmental rules or policies, that provision shall be interpreted or modified to the extent necessary to comply with such rules or policies, and the remaining provisions of this Constitution shall continue in full force and effect.

### 12.5 Governing Language

This Constitution shall be maintained in English as its official language. If an official Bangla translation is prepared for reference or internal use, the English version shall prevail in the event of any inconsistency or conflict between the two versions.

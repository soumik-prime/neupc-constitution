## Article 12 — Dissolution

### 12.1 Grounds

The Club may be dissolved:

1. upon withdrawal or termination of the Club's recognition by the University or the Department of Computer Science & Engineering; or
2. by a resolution approved by a three-quarters (3/4) majority of General Members present and voting at a specially convened General Assembly meeting called for the purpose of considering dissolution.

### 12.2 Proposal for Voluntary Dissolution

A proposal to dissolve the Club may be initiated by:

1. a written resolution supported by at least one-third (1/3) of the Executive Committee; or
2. a written petition signed by at least one-third (1/3) of General Members in good standing.

The proposal shall state the reasons for dissolution and shall be submitted to the General Secretary. If the General Secretary is unavailable or directly involved in the proposal, it shall be submitted to the President. If the President is also directly involved, the proposal shall be submitted to the Advisory Panel.

### 12.3 Notice of Dissolution Meeting

Upon receiving a valid proposal for voluntary dissolution, a General Assembly meeting shall be specially convened to consider the proposal.

At least fourteen (14) days' notice shall be given. The notice shall clearly state that dissolution of the Club is the matter to be considered and shall include the reasons stated in the proposal.

### 12.4 Quorum

The quorum for a General Assembly meeting considering dissolution shall be the quorum established under Clause 8.5.

If quorum is not met, the meeting shall be adjourned and reconvened in accordance with Clause 8.5. The dissolution resolution shall not be decided unless the required quorum is present at the meeting at which the vote is taken.

### 12.5 Approval of Dissolution

A resolution for voluntary dissolution shall require the approval of at least three-quarters (3/4) of General Members present and voting at the properly convened General Assembly meeting.

Voting shall be conducted by secret ballot unless otherwise permitted by this Constitution. The result shall be recorded in the official minutes of the meeting.

### 12.6 Institutional Confirmation

Where dissolution requires approval, confirmation, or withdrawal of recognition by the University or the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, the dissolution shall be completed in accordance with the applicable University and Departmental procedures.

The Executive Committee shall cooperate with the relevant institutional authority to complete any required formalities.

### 12.7 Dissolution Administration

Upon approval of voluntary dissolution, the Executive Committee shall remain in office only for the purpose of completing the dissolution process.

The President, General Secretary, and Treasurer shall oversee the process and may establish a temporary Dissolution Committee where necessary.

The responsible officers shall:

1. settle outstanding lawful liabilities;
2. secure and account for Club funds and property;
3. prepare the final financial statement and asset inventory;
4. transfer the Club's records, accounts, credentials, and other assets to the designated recipient; and
5. complete any other administrative requirements necessary for the formal closure of the Club.

### 12.8 Disposal of Assets

After settlement of all outstanding obligations, all remaining Club funds, property, equipment, digital assets, records, and other assets shall be transferred to the Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Netrokona University, or to another organization or body as permitted by applicable University or Departmental policies.

No member, officer, Advisor, or other individual shall receive or retain any portion of the Club's remaining funds or assets for personal benefit.

### 12.9 Final Records and Completion

The Treasurer shall prepare a final statement of the Club's financial position, including remaining funds, outstanding liabilities, and the disposition of assets. The final statement shall be reviewed in accordance with Clause 9.6 and submitted to the Department where required.

The General Secretary shall ensure that the Club's official records, minutes, financial records, asset records, and other essential documents are properly preserved or transferred in accordance with applicable University and Departmental requirements.

The dissolution shall be considered complete when all required institutional procedures have been completed, outstanding liabilities have been settled, and the Club's remaining assets and records have been properly transferred or disposed of.

Following completion of the dissolution, no person shall represent themselves as acting on behalf of the Club unless specifically authorized by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering or another competent University authority.

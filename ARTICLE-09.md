## Article 9 — Finance and Club Assets

### 9.1 Sources of Funds

Club funds may be derived from membership dues, University or Departmental grants, sponsorships, event revenue, voluntary donations, and other lawful sources approved in accordance with this Constitution.

### 9.2 Acceptance and Restriction of Funds

The Club shall not accept funds, sponsorships, donations, or other financial support from any source where acceptance would:

1. compromise the Club's independence, objectives, or reputation;
2. create an obligation to promote or support a political party, political organization, religious organization, or other external interest;
3. involve conditions inconsistent with this Constitution or the rules of the University; or
4. create an actual or perceived conflict of interest with the Club's activities or decisions.

Acceptance of financial support shall not grant the source any authority over the Club's decisions, activities, membership, or Executive Committee.

The Executive Committee shall review and approve or reject proposed sources of funding in accordance with this Section.

### 9.3 Custody

Club funds shall be maintained and safeguarded in a manner consistent with applicable University financial policies. Where a dedicated Club account is available, Club funds shall be maintained in that account. Until such an account is established, the Executive Committee shall designate an appropriate method for holding Club funds.

The Treasurer shall maintain accurate books and records of all Club funds and transactions.

No single Executive Member shall have sole control over Club funds or both authorize and disburse the same payment.

Any payment made to the Treasurer shall require the approval of the President. Any payment made to the President shall require the approval of the General Secretary.

### 9.4 Budget

The Treasurer shall prepare an annual budget at the beginning of each Executive Committee term. The budget shall be reviewed and approved by the Executive Committee and presented to the General Assembly for information.

### 9.5 Expenditure Approval

All Club expenditures shall be made in accordance with the approved annual budget. Any expenditure that is outside the approved budget or exceeds an approved budget allocation shall require prior approval by a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the Executive Committee.

The Treasurer shall maintain records of all approved expenditures and supporting documents.

### 9.6 Financial Review and Audit

The Club's accounts shall be reviewed at least once during each Executive Committee term. The review shall be conducted by members designated by the Executive Committee who are not responsible for maintaining the accounts. Where required by University or Departmental policy, the accounts shall also be subject to an external audit. The audit or financial review report shall be presented at the Annual General Meeting.

### 9.7 Financial Transparency

A summary of the Club's financial position and transactions shall be made available to any General Member upon reasonable request, subject to applicable University or Departmental policies and the protection of confidential or sensitive information.

### 9.8 Club Assets and Materials

All digital and physical assets acquired, created, or maintained for the Club, as well as materials produced in the course of Club activities, shall be considered Club property and shall not be treated as the personal property of any individual member or officer.

Such assets and materials include, but are not limited to, social media accounts, email accounts, source-code repositories, contest and judging platforms, domain names, websites, cloud storage, equipment, documents, graphics, photographs, videos, publications, software, source code, designs, presentations, and other creative or technical works.

The General Secretary shall maintain an up-to-date record of the Club's assets and their responsible custodians. Access credentials shall be stored and managed securely and shall be transferred to the incoming Executive Committee as part of the official handover.

The Club shall retain the right to use, modify, reproduce, publish, and distribute Club-created materials for its activities and purposes. Appropriate attribution shall be given to the original creator where reasonably practicable.

No member or officer shall retain exclusive control or claim exclusive ownership of a Club asset or Club-created material after leaving the relevant position or the Club.

### 9.9 External Agreements

No member may enter into a contract, memorandum of understanding, sponsorship agreement, or other binding external commitment on behalf of the Club without prior approval of the Executive Committee.

The President shall sign such agreements on behalf of the Club. The Treasurer may sign agreements that are purely financial and administrative in nature, provided that the agreement has been approved by the Executive Committee.

Any agreement involving a financial commitment outside the approved budget shall require prior approval by a two-thirds (2/3) majority of the Executive Committee.

No individual member or officer shall have authority to independently bind the Club to any external commitment.
